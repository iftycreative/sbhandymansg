Do you provide 24/7 handyman and emergency repairs? expand_more

Yes! SB Handyman SG operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week to support emergency repairs (active pipe bursts, power trips, jammed door locks) as well as scheduled home maintenance across Singapore.

How fast can a technician arrive at my home? expand_more

For urgent repair dispatches, our nearest mobile technician typically arrives within 30 to 45 minutes across Central, East, West, and North Singapore areas.

Can I get a quote before the work begins? expand_more

Yes. You can use our Quick Quote Estimator or send photos of your repair issue directly to our WhatsApp at +65 8116 9455. We will provide an upfront quotation before scheduling.

What types of home repairs do you cover? expand_more

We provide full-spectrum services including plumbing, electrical work, wall painting, aircon servicing, TV & fixture mounting, bathroom waterproofing/sealant, door and window locks, cabinet hinge repair, and general home repairs.

Do you service both HDB flats and Condos? expand_more

Yes, we service HDB flats, condominiums, private apartments, landed properties, and commercial retail/office spaces islandwide.

Yes, all our repair work is backed by a workmanship warranty. We test all completed jobs thoroughly with you before concluding the visit.
